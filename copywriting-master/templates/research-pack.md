# Research Pack Template

Use this template to synthesize the outputs of the three Phase 1 research agents (IVOC, Competitor, Product) into a single document that gets passed to the Strategist agent in Phase 2.

This is NOT where each agent writes their individual output — they each produce their own document. This is where those three outputs GET SYNTHESIZED into something coherent.

## How to use this template

1. **Run the 3 research agents in parallel** (IVOC, competitor, product). Each produces its own raw output.
2. **Read all three outputs.** Don't skim — nuance matters.
3. **Fill sections 1, 2, 3** with distilled versions of each agent's output. Don't just copy-paste — SUMMARIZE with the strategic intent in mind.
4. **Fill section 4 — Cross-Synthesis** carefully. This is where strategic insight is made. Look for alignments you wouldn't have seen without all three research streams.
5. **Note unknowns and gaps** in section 5.
6. **Adjust RIOA** if the research revealed something that changes the reader profile or idea direction.
7. **Hand off to the Strategist agent** along with the refined RIOA.

## What a good research pack looks like

- 3-6 pages total
- Every section is filled or explicitly marked "N/A — reason"
- Cross-synthesis section contains 3-5 strong alignments, not just one
- Verbatim IVOC quotes are preserved (vocabulary for the writer to pull from)
- Red lines are explicit and short

## Common failure modes

- **Research pack is just a concatenation of three agent outputs.** The cross-synthesis is the real value; don't skip it.
- **Cross-synthesis is generic.** Alignments must be SPECIFIC to this product/reader/market, not platitudes.
- **Red lines missing.** Always list them explicitly — vagueness here causes copy that tanks.
- **Proof inventory and gaps not distinguished.** Claims without proof are liabilities.
