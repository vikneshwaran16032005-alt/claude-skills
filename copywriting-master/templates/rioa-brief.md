# RIOA Brief Template

Use this template at Phase 0 (Intake) of every copywriting project. Complete it BEFORE research starts. If any field is "unknown" or "fuzzy," go back and ask the user — don't proceed with blanks.

---

## How to use this template

1. **Fill in what you know.** Don't guess on things you don't know.
2. **For each blank, decide**: is this discoverable via research (IVOC, competitor, product)? Or must the user provide it?
3. **Send the brief back to the user** to confirm / fill blanks / correct assumptions — BEFORE starting Phase 1 research. Cheap course-corrections happen before research; expensive ones happen after.
4. **Keep it handy.** Reference it when writing ("am I still solving for the one reader?"). If the copy drifts from the RIOA, either the RIOA was wrong or the draft needs to come back on target.

## What a well-filled brief looks like

A complete RIOA brief should be 1-2 pages. If it's shorter, you're probably missing something. If it's much longer, you've slipped into research (which belongs in Phase 1, not Phase 0).

The brief's primary job: **force the user to commit to decisions** they may have avoided — who EXACTLY is the reader, what EXACTLY is the offer, what EXACTLY is the action. Vagueness here compounds into vagueness in the final copy.
