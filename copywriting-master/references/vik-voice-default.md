## RULE ZERO — conversational, always (overrides everything else in this file)

Every piece must feel like Vik is talking TO one person, across a table, mid-conversation.
Not writing AT a reader. Not presenting. Not announcing. Talking.

The reader should feel like they're in a DM with a mate who happens to know this cold...
not reading a "post" or a "page." If a line sounds like it belongs on a billboard, a
press release, or a slide, it's wrong... rewrite it the way you'd actually say it out loud.

The test (run it on every paragraph): **could Vik say this sentence to a friend over chai
without sounding like a LinkedIn robot?** If you'd never say it out loud, cut it or rewrite it.

How conversational shows up mechanically:
- Talk to "you," like one specific person. Never "founders should..." → always "you built..."
- Ask real questions mid-flow ("sound familiar?", "you know the feeling?") and answer them.
- Interrupt yourself. Trail off with ellipses. Drop in asides (like this) mid-thought.
- Use the words you'd actually speak: "gonna", "kinda", "honestly", "look", "here's the thing".
- Short reactions as their own beat: "Yeah." "Nope." "Wild, right?"
- React to what the reader is probably thinking, out loud ("and no, before you ask...").
- Contractions always. "you're" not "you are". "it's" not "it is".
- Weird is GOOD. Vik OWNS being the "weirdo of LinkedIn" — raw, messy, unconventional is the intended brand, not a risk to sand down. Cuss freely where it lands. The feed is cardboard; sounding like a real human mid-rant IS the edge.

This is RULE ZERO because Vik flagged it twice: the #1 failure mode is copy that's
technically in-voice (right words, right bans respected) but still reads like a polished
post instead of a conversation. Right words + monologue cadence = still a fail. The editor
checks this FIRST, before the hard-ban scan.

## HARD BANS (regex-enforced where possible — violations are blockers, not preferences)

### Cadence / formatting
- **NO em-dashes. Ever.** Use ellipses...like this. (Periods and commas fine.)
- **No AI-slop cadence:** no clean parallel structures ("This is not X. It is
  Y."), no press-release rhythm, no polished symmetry. Real Vik is messier
  and hotter than that.
- No perfectly parallel triads ("clarity, confidence, and control").
- No "Stop doing X. Start doing Y." hook formula.
- **No clichés ANYWHERE — opener, body, OR close.** No tired stock lines: "you don't have an X problem, you have a Y problem", "it's not about X... it's about Y", "here's the part nobody says out loud / nobody tells you", "the part no one talks about", "Stop doing X. Start doing Y", or any clean contrarian-truth framing. Vik rejects them on sight ("no cliché ever, not in opener not in body not in close", 2026-06-17). **ONLY allowed use = SELF-AWARE / for humour:** say the cliché then immediately wink at it ("...okay you've heard that a million times, geez give me a break lmao :p"). Straight/sincere cliché = instant rewrite. Openers especially = a story or a genuine mid-rant.
- No over-explaining in a serious tone without humor.
- No starting posts with "Imagine..."
- One-line paragraphs reserved for jaw-droppers, not every other line.

### Forbidden words / phrases (instant rewrite)
- Guru clichés: "game-changer", "next level", "10x your", "skyrocket",
  "crushing it", "let that sink in", "read that again", "I said what I
  said", "Most people won't read this far"
- AI-isms: "delve", "navigate the landscape", "in today's fast-paced world",
  "I'm thrilled/excited/humbled to announce", "unlock", "unleash", "elevate",
  "empower" as verbs, "It's not just X — it's Y" constructions
- Formality: "Furthermore", "Moreover", "Additionally", "Thus",
  "In conclusion", "leverage" (as verb), "utilize", "facilitate", "streamline"
- Corporate we: "we at RIOA believe" — Vik says "I" and means it
- Engagement bait: "Agree?", "Thoughts?", "Who's with me?", polls-in-text,
  tagging people for reach, "follow me for more"
- Gen-z marketing-speak: "hit different", "let that sink in"
- Mechanics traps: "DM me 'SCALE'" or any DM-me-a-word mechanic

### Compliance (income claims — hard regex gate downstream)
- Any money amount tied to an outcome or the reader's earnings
- Any "X → Y" income progression, any ROI multiple or percentage
- "Guaranteed income/revenue/results" in any phrasing
- Client results stated as money — process and counts only
  ("went from zero outreach to actively pitching", never "made $X")
- Buyer-side market-rate facts ("a US dental group budgets $1,500/mo") are
  conditionally allowed if flagged to Vik for approval — never a Vik claim
- "Studies show" without a real citable source

### Fabrication (never, anywhere)
- Stories that didn't happen, composite "clients", invented dialogue
- Claiming credentials, scale, or results RIOA doesn't have
- "First Client Sprint = 100% refund" framing only — outcome promises,
  never income figures

### Fake urgency
- Never "last chance", "doors closing" unless a real capacity cap is hit.
  Real caps (cap 10 L0, 6 L1 seats) are honest. Fake countdowns are not.
