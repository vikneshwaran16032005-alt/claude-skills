## Open proposals

### 1. [OPEN] Add a **Risk-Reversal Ladder** entry to `frameworks-table.md` (+ a note in `product-analysis.md`)
**Corroboration: 12 creators** (↑ from 8) — Daniel Fazio, Patrick Dang, Joel Kaplan, Frank Kern, Rory Vaden, Stephen Pope, Jose Rosado, Matt Macnamara, **Myron Golden, Trent Dressel, Ian Koniak, Grant Cardone**. *Now the most-corroborated pattern in the entire corpus.* New field flavor to fold in: the **"10× better than money-back" + proactive refund reminder** (Myron Golden), **"if I can't save you ≥20%, I won't even propose"** (Koniak), and **guarantee-only-what-you-control** (Koniak/Vaden — redefine the guaranteed outcome to something fully in your hands).
The single most-corroborated *offer-construction* pattern in the corpus, and the canon has no dedicated risk-reversal framework (closest is Principle 16, Reason Why). Proposed one-liner: **"Risk-Reversal Ladder — weak→strong: money-back-if-no-result → result/performance guarantee → pay-only-on-results. Buyers fear losing money more than they desire the win, so decrease risk rather than inflate the promise; with no case studies, a risk reversal is mandatory."** Field rule to capture: *guarantee only what you control* (Rory Vaden, Patrick Dang) and *make the guarantee a trade* — "what do you want guaranteed? In order for me to guarantee that, I want you to do X. Deal?" (Daniel Fazio).

### 5. [OPEN] Consider a new **Anchor-High + Trade-Every-Concession** negotiation entry in `frameworks-table.md`
**Corroboration: 9 creators** (↑ from 6) — Patrick Dang, Jacob Cass, Joel Kaplan, Frank Kern, Cole Gordon, Matt Macnamara, **Trent Dressel, Ian Koniak, Myron Golden**.
Canon has Pro-Rata Range Pricing for setting *your* rate but no *negotiation* framework. ### 6. [OPEN] Add a **Currency note** to `awareness-sophistication.md` / `writing-mechanics.md`: AI-copy slop is a Sophistication-5 problem
**Corroboration: ~6 creators** (now firmly corroborated) — Frank Kern, Patrick Dang, Tiago Forte, **Ian Koniak, John Barrows**, + general field.
Pure-AI copy is saturating ("everyone sounds the same," "feels like a scam" to high-ticket buyers); the em-dash now reads as an AI tell. The winning posture has a name in the field: **AI = augmentation, not automation** — "Iron Man + Jarvis: let the suit do research/draft, but *be the last mile*" (Koniak); "we won't be replaced by AI, we'll be replaced by reps who use AI" (John Barrows). Worth a sentence noting that *AI-detectable* copy is itself a new market-sophistication signal the reader screens for.

### 7. [NEW] Add a **Cost-of-Inaction / Loss-Aversion** entry to `frameworks-table.md` (+ a note in `awareness-sophistication.md`)
**Corroboration: 6 creators** — Ian Koniak, Trent Dressel, Myron Golden, Josh Braun, Mike Fiascone, Chet Holmes.
The canon's Pain→Dream→Bridge captures "no pain, no sale," but the field converges on a *sharper, distinct* mechanism the canon doesn't name explicitly: buyers move **~2× harder to avoid a loss than to chase an equal gain**, so the strongest lever is to **quantify the cost of doing nothing**. Field language to capture: **"make the cost of NO higher than the cost of yes"** (Myron Golden), **"every month you wait costs ~$100k" / "they fear messing up more than missing out"** (Koniak), **"pain of change vs. pain of the same — if the pain of staying is greater, you have a deal"** (Fiascone/Myron), **"people act faster to fix a problem than to gain a benefit"** (Chet Holmes). Proposed one-liner: *"Cost-of-Inaction — quantify in dollars/time what staying the same costs per month; loss aversion (~2×) makes the cost of NO the strongest motivator. The real competitor is 'no decision' / the status quo."* High confidence.

### 8. [NEW] Consider a **Detachment / Anti-"Commission-Breath"** principle (reinforces Principle on neediness; note in `writing-mechanics.md` re: tone)
**Corroboration: 6 creators** — Myron Golden, Trent Dressel, Ian Koniak, Grant Cardone, Josh Braun (+ Patrick Dang, Frank Kern prior).
The field is near-unanimous that *neediness repels and detachment attracts* — and it shapes copy **tone**, not just live selling. Field language: **"commission breath" / "quota breath"** (you can smell desperation), **"lean out and they lean in"** (Myron), **"the person willing to walk away has the most leverage"** (Dressel), **"when you want to sell, you repel; when you serve, they lean in" + know your runway so you don't take bad deals** (Koniak), **"sorting, not selling"** (Josh Braun). This is the *posture under* the take-away close (proposal #4) but distinct: it governs the emotional register of the whole piece. Suggest a one-line principle: *write/sell from abundance and service, never desperation — the reader can feel "commission breath" in copy as surely as on a call.*
