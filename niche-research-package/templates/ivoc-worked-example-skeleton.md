## Note on this document

This is a worked example of an IVOC research deliverable, built using the methodology in the companion guide. It is **what good looks like** — a reference template for what depth, structure, and citation discipline a finished IVOC artefact should hit.

Every quote in this document is **verbatim** and traceable to a public URL. No quote has been paraphrased, smoothed, or fabricated. Where a quote was reported in a search summary but I couldn't independently verify the literal wording at the source URL, I have **excluded it** rather than risk distortion — even where it would have made the bucket fuller. Better fifteen real quotes than fifty plausible ones.
