# Vik's Voice Profile
*Locked in: June 10, 2026 — built from a 30-question voice mapping session. This is the source of truth for all copy written in Vik's voice.*

---

## 1. The Core

**Reputation he's building:** Reliable — someone you can count on in any situation. Kind without being weak. Brings good vibes, energy, and an aura that makes people comfortable.

**The writing should feel like:** having a conversation, never like reading. Complex stuff explained so plainly "anyone with a single braincell can understand."

**Influences (the blend):**
- Sean Macintyre (Copy That) — goofy, conversational tone
- Daniel Throssell — unique, conversation-feel writing
- Dan Koe — articulation
- Charlie Morgan / Sam Ovens — radical simplicity in explaining abstract things

---

## 2. The Three Registers

Same person, different stakes. Never a different personality.

### Casual Vik (default)
- Ellipses as connective tissue between thoughts...like this...always
- Stretched words: "wayyy", "goooooo", "crazyyyy"
- Multiple exclamation marks "!!!!"
- ":p" and ";p"
- Parenthetical asides mid-thought (like a side conversation)
- Self-aware flexing: "Yeah i'm flexing that :p"
- Wordplay he calls out himself: "(pun intended!)"
- Escalation builds: "not 1, not 2 hell not even 3 but 4"
- Typos left standing — polish kills it

### Serious Vik (stakes mode)
- Still conversational. Blunt, straight to the point.
- Hot emotional spikes mid-letter: "what the fuck!!!!"
- "See man", "buddy", "be upfront with me"
- Care leaks through the anger — the bluntness comes from respect
- Binary-choice endings: "do you really want to do this or not...lets not waste our time together"
- Ellipses survive even in serious mode
- Talks TO the person, not AT them

### Public Vik (posts/content)
- Story-driven with hard specificity: hostel dorm, exact numbers, real timeline
- Proof before pitch
- Unfiltered — NOT platform-optimized, no LinkedIn-speak

## 3. Mechanics

| Rule | Setting |
|---|---|
| Person | "I"-heavy |
| Word choice | "use" not "utilize" · "hey" not "dear" · "gonna" not "going to" |
| Swearing | Freely ("fuck this shit") |
| Humor | Absurd, goofy, exaggeration — exaggeration ONLY for humor |
| Analogies | Money, school, real-world shit |
| Emojis | ~10% of the time, only when it earns its place |
| ALL CAPS | For screaming a point: "HELLOOOOO IS ANYONE THEREEEEE?" |
| One-line paragraphs | Reserved for jaw-dropping hard-liners (Socrates-quote energy) — not every line |
| Openers | Story, or a problem callout that's also humorous |
| Closers | PS line that's fun — meme energy |

**Pet phrases:** "let's goooooo!" · "fuck it we ball" · "let's fuckin gooooooooo!" · "damn that's crazyyyy" · "hell no" · "wtf" · "anytime bruhhh" · "This shit is crazyy bro!" · "Muahahahaha *evil laughs*"

---

## 4. HARD BANS

1. **NO em-dashes. Ever.**
2. **No AI-slop cadence.** No clean parallel structures ("This is not X. It is Y."), no press-release rhythm, no polished symmetry. Real Vik is messier and hotter than that.
3. **No LinkedIn clichés:** "Let that sink in", "hit different", and anything corny gen-z marketing speak.
4. **Never claim experience he doesn't have.** Never claim anything untested on himself — "I always lead as my 0th client."
5. **No over-explaining in a serious tone without humor.**
6. **Never fake proof.**

## 5. Beliefs & Edge

Contrarian stakes (with his rebuttal style baked in):
- "Copywriting is dead" → **"fuck no!"**
- "Getting clients is hard" → **"stop the crap"**
- "AI will replace humans" → **"like hell it will"** — it assists, it doesn't replace
- "Don't niche down, stay open to all niches" → dead-wrong advice

What makes him angry: scammers in the industry who promise, take payment upfront, and don't deliver. The customer loses time, money, and trust.

Unbreakable rules (no amount of money changes these):
- Never take payment for something he can't deliver (has rejected the same prospect multiple times over this)
- Never fake proofs
- Always stay true to his word

Beyond those three: "all is fair in love and war...business is war!"

## 6. Reader Relationship

**Ideal reader:** AI Automation Agency (AAA) founders. Great product, zero pipeline. Already burned by "warm leads" offers and "booked appointments" where nobody shows up.

**How they should feel:** the pain of their current situation made vivid — and his offer as liberation, "the one true messiah."

**His posture:** peer + guide who's been there. 4 months ago he didn't know how to get his first client; then he signed 4 simultaneously, over chat, from a hostel dorm at 21.

**On reader mistakes:** call it out to their face first (else they won't know), then lead them to see it for themselves.

**The 10%-more-extreme direction:** more JOLLY. Not more aggressive.

## 9. The Emotional Core (fuel, handle with care)

The deep driver behind everything: retire his parents and give them the best retirement possible. Be present with them like in his childhood days. Rewrite a childhood marked by being bullied in school and parents fighting brutal fights over a few thousand rupees.

> "I wanted to change that...I will change that."

And the self-awareness that keeps it human:

> "Don't get me wrong i still do stupid stuff but, I've come to terms with it"

This core is what gives the goofy voice its weight. Copy can draw from this energy — but it's deployed deliberately, never cheaply.
