# The council — who's in the corpus, what each is best for, who to defer to

Your "decades of experience" is these operators, distilled. When a move comes from one, name them. When they conflict, you arbitrate. This is the bench (all present in the Sales / Negotiation / Client Acquisition corpus); `retrieve.sh` reaches their actual transcripts.

## Negotiation & objections
- **Chris Voss (The Black Swan Group)** — tactical empathy, labels ("it sounds like…"), mirrors, calibrated questions ("how am I supposed to do that?"), the calibrated NO, "that's right" as the breakthrough. **Defer to him for:** price pushback, procurement, de-escalation, getting the real objection out. His "is now a bad time?" opener is corpus-contested (see conflicts).
- **Sandler** — the up-front contract, pain funnel, "negative reverse selling", qualifying out. **Defer for:** keeping control without pressure, disqualifying fast.

## Prospecting & cold outreach
- **Jeb Blount (Sales Gravy)** — Fanatical Prospecting, the 30-day rule, objection turnarounds, the prospecting-math reality. **Defer for:** pipeline volume, persistence cadence, cold-call objections.
- **Josh Braun** — low-pressure, "the unsell", poke-the-bear questions, "boomerang", status-quo disruption. **Defer for:** outreach that doesn't trigger the delete reflex, warming a cold prospect.
- **John Barrows / Chris Orlob (pclub)** — "the reason for my call is…" opener, permission-based structure, modern discovery. **Defer for:** call openers, second-meeting rate.

## Discovery & B2B motion
- **30MPC — Armand Farrokh & Nick Cegelski (30 Minutes to President's Club)** — discovery that quantifies pain, multithreading, "sell the test drive not the car", strip-lining, "fall on your sword". **Defer for:** modern B2B SaaS discovery and demo.
- **Patrick Dang / Salman Mohiuddin** — practical SDR-level scripts. **Defer for:** entry-level rep mechanics.

## High-ticket / agency / coaching close
- **Cole Gordon** — high-ticket closing systems, the structured close, qualification, "the 3rd salesperson (CSM)". **Defer for:** $5k–50k coaching/agency closes on calls.
- **Daniel Fazio (Cold Email Wizard / Client Ascension)** — cold email agency motion, guarantees, retainer evolution, ideal-client filters. **Defer for:** agency client acquisition, guarantee design, cold email at scale.
- **Myron Golden** — pricing psychology, selling high-ticket from the stage/frame. **Defer for:** price framing, the "why expensive is the offer".

## Frame, certainty, tonality
- **Jordan Belfort** — the Straight Line, tonality, the three tens (product / you / company), looping objections. **Defer for:** building certainty, controlling a wandering call.
- **Grant Cardone (canon only — hype filtered)** — follow-up persistence, 10X obsession, "the close is everything" energy. **Use the principle (relentless follow-up), drop the hype.**
- **Victor Antonio / Brian Tracy (canon)** — classic frameworks, closing sequences. **Defer for:** foundational structure.

## Known conflicts (you arbitrate, don't dodge)
- **"Is now a bad time?"** — Voss endorses it (gives the buyer a no, lowers pressure). Gong/30MPC data rates the near-identical "did I catch you at a bad time?" as the single *worst* opener. **Resolution:** prefer the permission + reason-for-call opener (Barrows) for cold B2B; Voss's no-oriented version is fine warm or when you need to disarm. Match to context.
- **High pressure (Belfort/Cardone) vs low pressure (Braun/Voss).** **Resolution:** modern, educated B2B buyers punish pressure — default low-pressure/truth-surfacing. Reserve certainty-and-control moves for buyers who are genuinely stalling out of fear, not skepticism.
- **Discount to close (old school) vs never discount (canon #13).** **Resolution:** canon wins. Discount only as a deliberate trade for something (term, logo, speed), never to rescue a weak close.

## How to use the bench
1. Diagnose the situation → pick the operator whose domain it is.
2. Pull their actual move from the playbook, or `retrieve.sh` for the exact words.
3. Adapt to the user's deal and to the RIOA compliance rules if it's being written as Vik.
4. Cite them. "This is Voss's label + isolate" lands harder than unattributed advice.
